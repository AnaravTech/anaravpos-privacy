Delete Your ANARAVPOS Account

Users of ANARAVPOS can request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your ANARAVPOS account, please send an email to:

info@anaravtech.com

Please include:

  • Your registered name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your registered mobile number, if applicable
  • The subject: Account Deletion Request

What data will be deleted

After verification of the account owner, we may delete:

  • User account information
  • Name and contact information associated with the account
  • Authentication-related account data
  • Other personal data associated with the account, where applicable

Data that may be retained

Certain business, invoice, transaction, tax, GST, accounting, or legal records may be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Deletion timeframe

Account deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable timeframe after identity and account ownership verification.

Contact

For questions about account deletion, contact: info@anaravtech.com
