Delete Data - ANARAVPOS

ANARAVPOS provides users with options to delete certain business and application data without requiring them to delete their ANARAVPOS account.

How to delete shop data

Users can delete their shop or business data directly from the ANARAVPOS application using the available Delete Shop feature.

After deleting a shop, the user's ANARAVPOS account may remain active, allowing the user to create or manage another shop where supported.

Data that may be deleted

Depending on the selected deletion feature, the following data may be deleted:

  • Shop or business information
  • Shop address and contact information
  • Product information
  • Inventory and stock information
  • Customer information associated with the shop
  • Shop-related business data

Account information

Deleting shop or business data does not necessarily delete the user's ANARAVPOS account.

Users who want to permanently delete their ANARAVPOS account can submit an account deletion request through our account deletion page.

Request Account Deletion

Data Retention

Certain transaction, invoice, tax, GST, accounting, or other records may be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate business record-keeping requirements.
