Privacy Policy

ANARAVPOS

Last updated: September 4, 2026

ANARAVPOS ("we", "our", or "us") provides a Point of Sale (POS), billing, inventory, invoicing, and business management application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the ANARAVPOS mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, ANARAVPOS may collect and process the following information.

Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may process:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials
  • User account identifier

Authentication and account-related services may be provided through Supabase or other authorized service providers.

Shop and Business Information

ANARAVPOS may store information required to operate your business, including:

  • Shop or business name
  • Shop address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • GSTIN or other tax information
  • Business logo

Product and Inventory Information

To provide POS and inventory functionality, the app may process:

  • Product names
  • Product codes
  • Barcodes
  • HSN codes
  • Product prices
  • Stock and inventory information
  • Product images

Customer Information

When entered by the business user, ANARAVPOS may process:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Customer GSTIN or tax information

This information is used to create invoices, manage sales, and maintain customer and transaction records.

Orders, Sales and Invoice Information

ANARAVPOS may process business transaction information, including:

  • Order details
  • Products purchased
  • Quantities
  • Prices
  • Discounts
  • Tax and GST information
  • Invoice numbers
  • Payment-related transaction information entered by the business user
  • Invoice and receipt records

2. Camera Access

ANARAVPOS may request access to your device camera.

The camera is used for application features such as barcode scanning.

Camera access is not intended to be used for unrelated purposes.

3. Photos and Media Access

ANARAVPOS may request access to photos or media stored on your device.

This access may be used to allow you to select or upload:

  • Product images
  • Shop or business logos
  • Other images selected by you within the application

ANARAVPOS accesses images only when required for the feature you choose to use.

4. Microphone and Speech-to-Text

If you use speech-to-text or voice input features, ANARAVPOS may request microphone access.

Microphone access may be used to convert voice input into text for supported application features.

ANARAVPOS does not intend to use the microphone for background recording.

5. Bluetooth and Device Connections

ANARAVPOS may request permission to connect with supported nearby devices.

These devices may include:

  • Bluetooth thermal printers
  • Other supported printing devices

Bluetooth and device connection permissions are used to:

  • Discover compatible devices
  • Connect to supported printers
  • Print invoices
  • Print receipts
  • Provide other supported printing functionality

Bluetooth permissions are not intended to be used for unrelated tracking or location purposes.

6. Printer Connections

ANARAVPOS may connect to supported printers using technologies such as:

  • Bluetooth
  • USB, where supported
  • Network or system printing, where supported

These connections are used to print invoices, receipts, and other business documents.

7. How We Use Information

We may use collected or provided information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide POS and billing functionality
  • Manage products and inventory
  • Generate invoices and receipts
  • Calculate GST and other applicable taxes
  • Manage customer and sales records
  • Synchronize business information where applicable
  • Provide barcode scanning
  • Support image uploads
  • Support speech-to-text features
  • Connect to supported printing devices
  • Print receipts and invoices
  • Improve application functionality and reliability
  • Provide customer support

8. Data Storage and Security

Business and application data may be stored locally on your device and/or securely synchronized using cloud services such as Supabase.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. Data Sharing

ANARAVPOS does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, including providers for:

  • Authentication
  • Database services
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Infrastructure and hosting

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of ANARAVPOS, its users, or the public.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Business and application data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide application functionality, maintain business and transaction records, or meet applicable legal, tax, or accounting requirements.

Users may contact us regarding their account or data.

11. Children's Privacy

ANARAVPOS is designed as a business and Point of Sale application and is not intended for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
