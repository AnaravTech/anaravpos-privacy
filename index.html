Privacy Policy

ANARAVPOS ("we", "our", or "us") provides a Point of Sale (POS), billing, inventory, and business management application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the ANARAVPOS mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, ANARAVPOS may collect and process the following information.

Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may process:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials
  • User account identifier

Authentication and account-related services may be provided through Supabase.

Shop and Business Information

The app may store information required to operate your business, including:

  • Shop or business name
  • Shop address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • GSTIN or other business tax information
  • Business logo

Product and Inventory Information

To provide POS and inventory functionality, the app may process:

  • Product names
  • Product codes
  • Barcodes
  • HSN codes
  • Product prices
  • Stock and inventory information
  • Product images

Customer Information

When entered by the business user, the app may process:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Customer GSTIN or tax information

Customer information is used to create invoices, manage sales, and maintain transaction records.

Orders, Sales and Invoice Information

ANARAVPOS may process business transaction information, including:

  • Order details
  • Products purchased
  • Quantities
  • Prices
  • Discounts
  • Tax and GST information
  • Invoice numbers
  • Payment-related transaction information entered into the app
  • Invoice and receipt records

2. Camera Access

ANARAVPOS may request camera access to provide barcode scanning functionality.

The camera is used to scan product barcodes and is not used for unrelated purposes.

3. Photos and Image Uploads

The app may allow users to select or upload images, including:

  • Product images
  • Shop or business logos

Images are used only to provide the selected application features.

4. Microphone and Speech-to-Text

If the speech-to-text feature is used, ANARAVPOS may request microphone access.

Microphone access is used to convert voice input into text for supported application features.

The microphone is not intended to be used for background recording.

5. Printer and Device Connections

ANARAVPOS may connect to supported printing devices, including:

  • Bluetooth printers
  • USB printers
  • Network or system printers

These connections are used to print invoices, receipts, and other business documents.

Bluetooth or device connection information is used only for providing printing functionality.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide POS and billing functionality
  • Manage products and inventory
  • Generate invoices and receipts
  • Calculate GST and other applicable taxes
  • Manage customer and sales records
  • Synchronize business information where applicable
  • Provide barcode scanning
  • Print receipts and invoices
  • Improve application functionality and reliability
  • Provide customer support

7. Data Storage and Security

Business and application data may be stored locally on your device and/or securely synchronized using cloud services such as Supabase.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Data Sharing

ANARAVPOS does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as authentication, database, cloud synchronization, and infrastructure providers.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect the rights, security, and integrity of our application and users.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Business data may be retained for as long as it is necessary to provide the application's functionality or comply with applicable legal and accounting requirements.

Users may contact us to request information regarding their account or data.

10. Children's Privacy

ANARAVPOS is designed as a business and Point of Sale application and is not intended for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.
